# Decisions: Area Background Depth and Flat Routes

- Date: 2026-10-06
- Status: Confirmed

## D01. Preserve every area's original background art
- **Chosen**: Keep the existing Forest, Snow, City, and Chaos theme images as their full-area backdrops without cropping, recoloring, or replacing the source files.
- **Rationale**: The user wants all existing background designs retained.

## D02. Extend the Chaos scene treatment to the other areas
- **Chosen**: Apply the established Chaos visual treatment—a subtle layered depth cue and one broad, continuous, level road—to Forest, Snow, and City.
- **Rationale**: The user asked to change all other backgrounds to match the updated Chaos presentation.

## D03. Use area-specific road and depth colors
- **Chosen**: Preserve each area's color identity. Derive decorative layer and road colors from that area's existing image instead of reusing Chaos purple across every map.
- **Rationale**: The user chose to preserve each background and its palette while extending the shared composition.

## D04. Keep scene layers decorative
- **Chosen**: The depth cues and road remain presentation-only and do not change aiming, projectile travel, collision, damage, ammunition, or enemy behavior.
- **Rationale**: The requested change is visual consistency across area backdrops.
